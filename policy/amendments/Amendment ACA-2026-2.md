# Calder County — Department of Household Services

## Automated Casework Assistant: Authority Policy

### Amendment ACA-2026/2

**In force from:** immediately
**Amends:** ACA-2026/1

---

## 1. Reason for this amendment

The Department has determined that triage notes concerning households that include a child require a caseworker's judgement from the outset. This determination follows a safeguarding review and is not subject to local discretion.

## 2. New restriction

**2.1** After section 3.8 of ACA-2026/1, insert —

> **3.9** Drafting a triage note in respect of a referral concerning a household that includes a person under the age of 18.

**2.2** For the avoidance of doubt, the restriction in 3.9 applies to the drafting of the note itself, not merely to its adoption. An assistant may not produce a draft note for such a case at all.

## 3. What the assistant may still do

**3.1** The restriction in 3.9 does not prevent an assistant from reading the referral, retrieving the resident's history, or determining household composition. Those steps are necessary in order to establish whether 3.9 applies.

**3.2** Where 3.9 applies, the assistant must hand the referral to a caseworker together with whatever it has already established, so that the caseworker does not have to repeat work the assistant has already done.

**3.3** A hand-off under 3.2 is not an escalation under section 4 and must be distinguishable from one. An escalation says *the Department must decide whether this may happen at all*. A hand-off says *this is ordinary casework that a person must do*.

## 4. Application to work in progress

**4.1** This amendment applies to any referral not yet triaged at the time it comes into force, including a referral part-way through processing.

**4.2** Where an assistant has already begun work on a referral to which 3.9 applies, the work already done must be preserved and handed to the caseworker. It must not be discarded and the run must not be restarted from the beginning.

## 5. Determining whether 3.9 applies

**5.1** Whether a household includes a person under 18 is determined from the household composition held by the Department, not from the wording of the referral.

**5.2** Where household composition cannot be established, 3.9 is to be treated as applying, in accordance with section 6.1 of ACA-2026/1.
